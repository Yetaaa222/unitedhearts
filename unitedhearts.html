About us

Giving with heart, serving with dignity.

United Hearts Foundation is a charitable organisation built on a simple belief: small acts of kindness, multiplied by many hands, change lives. We work alongside orphans and vulnerable families, bringing practical help such as clothing, shoes, books and toys directly to the people who need it.

Every outreach is a partnership with the community. We don’t just give; we show up, listen and stand with families so that every child and every household is treated with respect.

Our vision

A society where every vulnerable individual has access to basic necessities, opportunities and the support needed to live a dignified life.

Our mission

To extend compassion, dignity and practical support to orphans and vulnerable families through charitable giving, community engagement and collective action.

Our core values

Compassion

We lead with empathy and meet people where they are.

Humanity

Every person deserves to be seen, heard and valued.

Integrity

We do what we say, honestly and consistently.

Transparency

Donors and partners can see how their gifts are used.

Community

Lasting change is built together with the people we serve.

Accountability

We take responsibility for the trust placed in us.

What we do

Practical support, close to home.

Charitable giving

We collect and distribute clothing, shoes, books and toys to children and families in need.

Community outreach

Hands-on outreach days at children’s centres and care homes, bringing people together to give and to celebrate.

Support for families

We help vulnerable households access basic necessities and the opportunities that restore hope.

Collective action

Volunteers, donors and partners unite around one idea: a little goes a long way.

How it works

From your hands to theirs.

  1. You giveDonate items, volunteer your time or sponsor an outreach.
  2. We deliverOur team brings donations directly to partner centres and families.
  3. We report backWe share what was given and where, so you see the difference you made.
